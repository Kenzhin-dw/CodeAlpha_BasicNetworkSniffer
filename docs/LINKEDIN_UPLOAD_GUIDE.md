# Manual LinkedIn Video Upload Guide

No LinkedIn action is automated by this project.

1. Complete the manual GitHub upload first and copy the public repository URL.
2. Record the technical explanation by following `VIDEO_RECORDING_GUIDE.md` and `VIDEO_TRANSCRIPT_EN.md`.
3. Watch the complete recording before uploading. Confirm that the audio is understandable and no password, token, notification, private IP, adapter GUID, username, or unrelated packet data is visible.
4. Open LinkedIn in your browser and sign in manually.
5. Select **Start a post**.
6. Choose the video/media option and upload the recorded project explanation video.
7. Open `docs/LINKEDIN_POST.md` and copy its prepared English caption.
8. Replace `<PASTE_GITHUB_LINK_HERE>` with the real repository URL, for example:

   ```text
   https://github.com/<username>/<repository>
   ```

9. Review the caption, video preview, repository link, and hashtags. Do not describe yourself as a CodeAlpha employee.
10. Publish the post manually.
11. Open the published post itself.
12. Use LinkedIn's **Copy link to post** action, or copy the post URL from the browser.
13. Open the copied URL in a private/incognito browser window if possible. Confirm that it opens the specific published post and that the video is visible with the intended audience settings.

Keep the copied LinkedIn post URL for the CodeAlpha field **LinkedIn Video Explanation Post Link (Task 1)**. Do not submit your LinkedIn profile URL.
